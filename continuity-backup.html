Protection rules

Only schema version, timestamp, mode, queue and lessons are accepted. Queue items are limited to known Jarvis modules. Secret-like values, unknown keys, oversized records, unsupported modes and malformed data are rejected. This screen never handles credentials, tokens, payment data, device privileges or hidden access paths.
